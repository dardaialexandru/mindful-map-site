How mind and body connect

Five channels. Some are well supported. Others are only plausible, and we mark them that way.

  1. Breathing and nerves

    Slow breathing calms the body and raises heart rate variability. No technique is proven better than another.

    Shown to help in trials · Medium evidence

  2. Stress hormones

    Long-term stress changes the daily rhythm of stress hormones.

    Observed link only · Good evidence

  3. Inflammation

    In some people with depression there is mild inflammation. We do not know which causes which.

    Observed link only · Medium evidence

  4. The gut

    A link with mood is plausible, but the evidence is limited. Probiotics are not a proven treatment.

    Observed link only · Limited evidence

  5. Repeating thoughts

    Rumination and heavy worry raise pulse, blood pressure and stress hormones.

    Observed link only · Good evidence

Not on this map because they do not hold up: “stimulate your vagus nerve”, polyvagal theory as settled fact, oxytocin as the “love hormone”, and “feeling your heartbeat lowers anxiety”.

Everything here is general information from published research. It does not replace a doctor or therapist. Effects in this research are usually small or medium. Nothing here is a cure.
