Mindfulness

Paying attention to the present, without judging.

Shown to help in trials · Medium evidence

Mindfulness helps. In reviews it works about as well as other proven treatments such as therapy, though it is not better.

How to start

  • Start with five minutes of noticing your breath.
  • If it makes you feel worse, stop and talk to a professional.

Needs this serves: Control

Sources
