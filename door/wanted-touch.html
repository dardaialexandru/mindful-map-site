Wanted touch

Hugs, massage, closeness you want.

Observed link only · Medium evidence

In studies, touch such as massage or hugs lowers pain, anxiety and low mood, though the studies are small. Do not expect it to prevent illness.

How to start

  • Ask for a hug from someone you trust.

Needs this serves: TouchBelonging

Sources

  • Packheiser et al. 2024, touch interventions (full text)
  • Cohen et al. 2015, hugs and colds (Carnegie Mellon) (press release)
