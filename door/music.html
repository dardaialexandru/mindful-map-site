Music

Listening or making it.

Shown to help in trials · Medium evidence

Music lowers felt stress by a medium amount, and has a smaller effect on pulse, blood pressure and stress hormones.

How to start

  • Make a playlist for winding down.

Needs this serves: A safe place

Sources

  • de Witte et al. 2020, music and stress, 104 trials (abstract)
