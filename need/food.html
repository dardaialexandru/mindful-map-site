Food

What and how you eat.

Observed link only · Medium evidence

People who eat a lot of ultra-processed food have more anxiety and depression. That is a link only: we do not know which causes which.

One small trial found that help to eat better lifted mood in people with depression, but it was too small to be sure.

What you can try

  • Regular meals, mostly unprocessed food.
  • Do not treat food as a cure for low mood.

Feelings this may sit behind: Tired

Everything here is general information from published research. It does not replace a doctor or therapist. Effects in this research are usually small or medium. Nothing here is a cure.
