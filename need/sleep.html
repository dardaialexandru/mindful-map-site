Sleep

Enough, and at regular hours.

Shown to help in trials · Good evidence

Too little sleep raises anxiety and dulls good feelings. Its effect on low mood is less clear-cut. The link with anxiety is among the best supported on the map.

Regular hours matter as much as total hours: going to bed and getting up at similar times each day.

What you can try

  • Keep a fixed wake-up time, including weekends.
  • Get daylight in the morning.
  • If you cannot sleep for weeks, talk to a doctor.

Doors to try

  • Regular sleepShown to help in trials
  • Morning lightObserved link only
  • Conscious breathingShown to help in trials
