Intimacy and reproduction

Closeness, sex, and children for those who want them.

Observed link only · Medium evidence

Sexual satisfaction goes with better wellbeing, but it is a link, not a proven cause. For couples, the benefit rises up to about once a week and then levels off.

On average, having children makes parents neither happier nor unhappier: the joy is balanced by costs in money and time.

Stress before fertility treatment does not appear to lower its chances, so nobody should be blamed for it.

What you can try

  • Talk openly with a partner about needs.
  • See a doctor for fertility questions.

Everything here is general information from published research. It does not replace a doctor or therapist. Effects in this research are usually small or medium. Nothing here is a cure.
