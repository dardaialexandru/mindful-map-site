A safe place

Clean air, quiet, green space.

Observed link only · Limited evidence

Aircraft noise and pollution are linked with more depression. Green space and quiet are linked with better wellbeing. The evidence is thin to medium.

What you can try

  • Spend time in green space when you can.
  • Reduce noise where you sleep.

Doors to try

  • Time in natureObserved link only
  • MusicShown to help in trials

Everything here is general information from published research. It does not replace a doctor or therapist. Effects in this research are usually small or medium. Nothing here is a cure.
