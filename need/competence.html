Competence

Feeling capable.

Observed link only · Good evidence

Feeling able to do things goes with better wellbeing. Learning something and finishing it builds this.

What you can try

  • Pick a small skill and practise it.
  • Plan with “if this, then I do that”. This works modestly in trials.

Doors to try

  • Seeing a situation differentlyShown to help in trials
  • Cognitive behavioural therapyShown to help in trials
  • ExerciseShown to help in trials
