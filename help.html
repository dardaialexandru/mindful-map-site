Get help now

If you are in danger, or thinking of hurting yourself, do not wait. Talk to a person now.

Emergency: 112One free number for the whole European Union.

Crisis lines

  • Spain: 024, suicide prevention line. Free, confidential, open 24 hours.
  • Romania: DepreHUB 0374 456 420, free, open 24 hours, for adults. TelVerde Antisuicid 0800 801 200, Friday to Sunday, 19:00 to 07:00. Children and teens: 116 111, Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 20:00.
  • Germany: Telefonseelsorge 0800 111 0 111, 0800 111 0 222 or 116 123. Free, anonymous, open day and night, in German.

If your line is closed, or you are in danger, call 112.

Numbers change. We re-check them before each update. If a line does not answer, call 112.

Everything here is general information from published research. It does not replace a doctor or therapist. Effects in this research are usually small or medium. Nothing here is a cure.
