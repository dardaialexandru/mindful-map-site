Finding harmony in the middle

Harmony is not a fourth path. It is what appears when mind, body and spirit finally stop pulling in different directions.

Get the map, weekly

Three territories, one centre

We tend to live in one of them: always thinking, always moving, or always searching. Harmony is found where the three meet, and you reach it by giving each one a little attention, every day.

Mind

Notice the thought without following it. One minute between the impulse and the choice is enough to begin.

Body

Slow your breath, soften your shoulders, move a little. The body calms before the mind agrees to.

Spirit

Make room for silence and for the bigger questions. Even five quiet minutes remind you of what is underneath the noise.

A five-minute way to meet in the middle

  1. Body first. Sit down, drop your shoulders and take five slow breaths, longer out than in.
  2. Then the mind. Name what you are thinking about, without judging it, and let it pass.
  3. Then the spirit. Stay in silence for a minute and ask: what do I actually need right now?
  4. Repeat daily. Harmony is a habit, not a destination. A small ritual at the same time each day makes it easier to return to.
