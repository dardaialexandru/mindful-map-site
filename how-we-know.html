How we know

Only research that has been checked by other scientists goes on this map. Where the evidence is weak, we say so.

Two kinds of lines

Shown to helpTrials with a comparison group found a benefit.
Observed link onlyTwo things appear together. Cause is not shown.

Three levels of evidence

  • Good: several solid studies or reviews agree.
  • Medium: the direction is clear but not settled.
  • Limited: plausible, but few or conflicting studies.

What we leave out

Popular ideas that did not hold up when tested again are not on the map. Examples: habits forming in 21 days, willpower running out like a battery, “power poses”, and claims that trauma is written in your DNA.

What we do not know yet

Autism in adults, chronic illness and depression, trauma treatment in adults, and screens in adults. We say nothing about them until we have read the research.

Effects found in research are usually small or medium. What helps one person may not help another.

Everything here is general information from published research. It does not replace a doctor or therapist. Effects in this research are usually small or medium. Nothing here is a cure.
