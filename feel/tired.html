Feeling

Tired

Low on energy, heavy, drained. These needs are most often behind it. Start at the top.

See a doctor if it lastsIf tiredness lasts for weeks, ask a doctor. Thyroid problems, anemia and other medical causes are common and treatable.

  1. Sleep

    Enough, and at regular hours. The best-supported link on this page.

    Shown to help · solid evidence

  2. Movement

    Lifts mood in many small studies. The certainty is low.

    Some evidence · small studies, low certainty

  3. Rhythm and light

    Regular days and daylight, especially in the morning.

    Some evidence · mostly questionnaires

  4. Food

    Eating a lot of ultra-processed food goes with worse mood, but that is a link, not a proven cause.

    Observed link · a cause is not shown

How these fit together

The first stop, sleep, also connects to rhythm and light and to movement. Starting with one of them often makes the others easier.

In everyday life

Made-up examples to help you see yourself. They are not study results.

Ana, 34 is tired by 3 pm every day and blames her age. When she looks at the list, sleep timing and morning light are the two that fit her days.
Marco, 41 drinks coffee until late and wonders why nights are broken. Sleep and caffeine sit at the top of the list.
