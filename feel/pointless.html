Feeling

Without purpose

Life feels like it has no point. These needs may be behind it. Start at the top.

  1. Meaning

    A sense of purpose goes with less stress, and it may run both ways.

    Some evidence · a link, cause not shown

  2. Competence

    Feeling capable can give direction to your days.

    Some evidence · a link, cause not shown

  3. Belonging

    Being part of something with other people often brings meaning.

    Strong link in many studies · cause not proven

How these fit together

Meaning, competence and belonging connect. Doing something well for someone else often touches all three.
