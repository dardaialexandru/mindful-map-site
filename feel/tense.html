Feeling

Tense or anxious

On edge, racing thoughts, tight body. These needs may be behind it. Start at the top.

Talk to someone if it will not easeIf worry or panic stays strong for weeks, or gets in the way of daily life, a doctor or therapist can help, and it is very treatable.

  1. Sleep

    Better sleep went with less anxiety and stress in a review of 65 trials. The best-supported link here.

    Shown to help · solid evidence

  2. Movement

    Exercise lifted mood in 218 studies. Walking, yoga and strength training all helped.

    Some evidence · small studies, low certainty

  3. Touch

    Wanted touch went with lower anxiety in the studies, mostly in the short term.

    Some evidence · short-term effects

  4. A safe place

    Noise and clean air may play a part. The evidence is mostly observation.

    Some evidence · mostly observation

  5. Stability

    Money and work worries are a common source of strain.

    Solid for work and hours, some evidence for money and home

How these fit together

Sleep, movement and touch are body needs that can be tried at home. Stability is bigger and takes longer.
