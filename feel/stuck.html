Feeling

Stuck

Not moving forward, no way to decide. These needs may be behind it. Start at the top.

  1. Control

    A stronger sense of control goes with better mood.

    Some evidence · a link, cause not shown

  2. Competence

    Support to try and learn goes with feeling capable.

    Some evidence · a link, cause not shown

  3. Stability

    Steady money, work and housing give room to move.

    Solid for work and hours, some evidence for money and home

How these fit together

Control, competence and stability connect. A secure base makes choices easier, and small wins build the feeling of being able.
