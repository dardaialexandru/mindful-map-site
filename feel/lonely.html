Lonely

Far from people, or alone among them. These needs may be behind it. Start at the top.

    A few warm relationships mattered more than many distant ones.

    Wanted touch helps pain, anxiety and mood in the short term.

    Some evidence · short-term effects

    Not enough evidence yet · mostly summaries
