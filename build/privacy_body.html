Who we are

The Mindful Map (themindfulmap.org) is run by Alexandru Dardai, based in Spain. Contact: hello@themindfulmap.org.

What we collect and why

If you subscribe, through the optional popup or the form, we collect your email address, only to send you your free map (the Harmony Map), our weekly email about body, mind and spirit, and occasional recommendations. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by submitting the form. We do not ask for any other personal data.

Who processes it

  • MailerLite, our email service and data processor, stores your address and sends our emails. Its data is held in the European Union, and transfers outside it are covered by standard contractual clauses.
  • Netlify, our website host, keeps a backup copy of form submissions.

We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also write to us to have your data deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its use, or to withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to the address above. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD, aepd.es).

Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to the address above. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. The optional email popup keeps one small note in your browser, only after you close it or subscribe, so that it does not appear again the same day or after you subscribe. It counts your clicks only while the tab is open. Nothing about you is sent anywhere by these notes.
