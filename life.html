Life around you

The setting your needs live in. Same rule: we say how sure we are.

  1. Relationships

    Warm, varied ties go with better health, but the link is small and observed only. Couple therapy helps unhappy couples; short courses help little. For loneliness, face-to-face groups help in the short term. Most people who divorce do not become depressed; a minority need support.

    Observed link only · Medium evidence

  2. Work

    Burnout is a work-related state, not a medical diagnosis, and overlaps a lot with depression. Changing the work and building skills each give small gains, more together. Unemployment and very long hours do harm. Working from home depends on choice, amount and support.

    Observed link only · Medium evidence

  3. Habits

    A new habit takes months, not three weeks. Tracking your progress and “if this, then that” plans help modestly. Plan for the days you miss.

    Shown to help in trials · Medium evidence

  4. Trauma

    Hard events are very common. PTSD is much rarer, and most people recover. Guidelines recommend trauma-focused therapy with a specialist. This site does not give therapy steps.

    Observed link only · Medium evidence

  5. Screens and social media

    Small and uneven links, mostly studied in teenagers. For adults we do not know yet.

    Observed link only · Limited evidence

  6. Racing thoughts and ADHD

    A busy mind is very common and does not mean ADHD. Only a specialist can diagnose ADHD.

    Observed link only · Limited evidence

  7. Grief, alcohol and meaning

    Grief is normal and rarely turns into illness. Alcohol has no clear causal link to starting depression, but it worsens sleep and mood.

    Observed link only · Medium evidence
Deep and lasting low moodIf low mood is very low and lasts, ask for a clinical check, whatever label you use for it, including burnout.

Everything here is general information from published research. It does not replace a doctor or therapist. Effects in this research are usually small or medium. Nothing here is a cure.
