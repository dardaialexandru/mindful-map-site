Life around you

The setting your needs live in. Same rule: we say how sure we are.

  1. Relationships

    Warm, varied ties go with better health, but this is an observed link, not proof of cause. Couple therapy helps unhappy couples; short courses give small to moderate gains. For loneliness, face-to-face groups help in the short term. About two in three people who divorce show no lasting depression; roughly one in ten develops new depression and needs support.

    Observed link only · Medium evidence

  2. Work

    Burnout is a work-related state, not a medical diagnosis, and its symptoms can look like those of depression. Changing the work and building skills each give small gains, with weak evidence. Unemployment goes with worse mental health. Working 55 or more hours a week goes with a higher risk of stroke and, to a smaller degree, heart disease. Working from home depends on choice, amount and support.

    Observed link only · Medium evidence

  3. Habits

    A new habit takes months, not three weeks. Tracking your progress and “if this, then that” plans help by a small to medium amount. Plan for the days you miss.

    Shown to help in trials · Medium evidence

  4. Trauma

    Hard events are very common. PTSD is much rarer. Many people recover, and a notable share of cases ease within months. Guidelines recommend trauma-focused therapy with a specialist. This site does not give therapy steps.

    Observed link only · Medium evidence

  5. Screens and social media

    Small and uneven links, mostly studied in teenagers. For adults we do not know yet.

    Observed link only · Limited evidence

  6. Racing thoughts and ADHD

    A busy mind can have many causes. Only a specialist can diagnose ADHD.

    Observed link only · Limited evidence

  7. Grief, alcohol and meaning

    Grief is normal. For a minority, roughly one in ten or fewer, it becomes a long-lasting illness. Drinking more goes with a higher risk of depression, and some studies suggest it may contribute, but the cause is uncertain. Alcohol also worsens sleep and mood.

    Observed link only · Medium evidence
Deep and lasting low moodIf low mood is very low and lasts, ask for a clinical check, whatever label you use for it, including burnout.

Everything here is general information from published research. It does not replace a doctor or therapist. Effects in this research are usually small or medium. Nothing here is a cure.
